Where are heatwaves most frequent?

The map shows average annual heatwave days during the 2020s, using data from 2020–2025.

Heatwave duration by state and territory

Each state shows the average of its grid cells’ annual longest positive-EHF runs over 2020–2025. Cells and years receive equal weight. Hover for the maximum run and average heatwave days.

Results use available dates without annualisation; 2025 may be incomplete. Runs touching yearly file boundaries may be truncated. Grey areas have no represented grid cells.

Sources: CSIRO EHF; ABS ASGS 2021 state and territory boundaries (CC BY 4.0). Aggregation details.

Heatwave intensity at representative locations

Circle area shows maximum EHF; colour shows mean EHF across positive-EHF days in 2020–2025. EHF is an intensity index in °C², not air temperature. This compares eight locations, not the most intense cell anywhere in Australia.

Each location uses its nearest valid CSIRO land-grid centre (2.3–6.9 km from the city centre). Annual averages use available dates without annualisation; 2025 may be incomplete, and runs may be truncated at year boundaries.

Sources: CSIRO EHF; GeoNames city coordinates; ABS ASGS 2021 basemap (CC BY 4.0).
